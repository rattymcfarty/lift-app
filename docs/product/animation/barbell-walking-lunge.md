# Barbell Walking Lunge — `barbell-walking-lunge`

Status: brief   ·   Author: OpenCode helper   ·   Date: 2026-11-01

_Facts below are taken from the KB entry `barbell-walking-lunge` (bundle 0.6.0): name, equipment, primary/secondary muscles, instructions, cues, safety notes._

- **Pattern / kit**: lunge · barbell on the upper back + stepping feet (reference script: `scripts/animation/lift_squat.py`). Keep the squat's rack/platform set, the bar on the back (`BAR0` at z 1.53, `prof` contact measurement), the arm solve (`solve_side` / `wrap_fingers`), the back-bar gap check and the depth/tempo machinery. Change the ground plan: the ankles are no longer pinned — one foot steps, the hips travel, and each leg is solved against its own moving ankle target.
- **Closest done clip**: `barbell-squat` — same bar, same back contact, same camera family, same leg solver. What changes: one foot leaves the floor, the trunk stays upright instead of leaning, the depth is per-leg and asymmetric, the base of support moves, and the worked muscles are the quadriceps only (the squat also glows the glutes, which are secondary here).
- **Start / end pose**: loop start = end, standing. Bar on the upper back at the squat's measured `BAR0`, half grip 0.44 m; feet shoulder-width (±0.17 m per the KB instruction), toes forward, both soles flat; knees unlocked; trunk vertical (within 5°); eyes forward. Lunge bottom (lead leg = his left, first half of the loop): lead ankle 0.55 m forward of its mark, lead knee ~85–90°, lead shin 15–20° forward, lead sole flat; rear foot still on its mark with the heel 5 cm up and the ball down; rear knee 4–6 cm above the floor, rear hip extended; trunk vertical with a ≤8° forward lean; bar level.
- **Movement**: the implement does not travel relative to the body — the bar is clamped to the upper back and moves with the trunk, so the whole clip is driven by the feet and hips. Per half-cycle: lead foot swings forward 0.55 m and lands, hips drop ~0.35 m and travel forward ~0.30 m, then the lead leg drives and the rear foot swings through to become the new lead. Two mirrored half-cycles per 3 s loop, and each stepping foot returns to its own mark before the next half-cycle starts. Tempo (72 frames): 0–0.05 stand; 0.05–0.32 left step and descend; 0.32–0.40 bottom hold; 0.40–0.55 drive up and step-through; 0.55–0.80 right lunge down; 0.80–0.88 bottom hold; 0.88–1.0 drive up, feet back on the marks. A true walk-forward covers ~1.1 m per loop and would jump at the seam in a square auto-framed tile; the return-to-mark version keeps the step-through reading without the jump. Owner decision if he wants travel instead.
- **Supports** (must touch their surface): exactly one planted foot flat at a time (float ≤ 0.5 cm, sink ≤ 1.5 cm), except the passing instant when both are down; the rear foot on its ball has the heel ≥ 4 cm above the floor; the swinging foot clears the floor by ≥ 5 cm at its highest and never dips below the floor plane; the bar rests on the upper back (gap 0–0.6 cm, sink ≤ 1.5 cm — the squat's check, unchanged); both hands on the bar with the squat's grip tolerances.
- **Grip / hands**: the squat's grip, unchanged — both hands on the bar at half grip 0.44 m, palms forward over the bar, wrists neutral, fingers wrapped once and rigid for the whole loop. The bar does not move in the hands; the hands move with the trunk only.
- **Worked muscles (orange)**: primary — quadriceps, both thighs (the front of the thigh from hip to knee; the lead leg works hardest and the rear leg drives the extension). Secondary — calves, glutes, hamstrings (not highlighted; drop the squat's `glute` term, the KB files glutes as secondary for this entry). Reuse the squat's `quad` term exactly (t ∈ [0.30, 0.96] along the thigh axis, off.y < 0, narrowing toward the knee) on both legs, so the glow follows each thigh through the split.
- **Camera**: three-quarter front from his left — yaw 40°, pitch 8°. Reason: the quadriceps are on the front of the thigh, and the first lunge leads with his left leg, so the camera on his left keeps the working leg nearest and unscreened; from behind (yaw > 100°) the quads vanish. Extend the auto-framing `pts` list to both foot marks (front and rear) plus the lead foot at full stride, or the step is cropped.
- **Checks to add or relax** (with the reason; never weaken a rule just to pass):
  - **Replace** "both feet flat on the floor" with "one planted foot flat (float ≤ 0.5 cm, sink ≤ 1.5 cm); the other foot either on its ball with the heel ≥ 4 cm up, or ≥ 5 cm clear of the floor and never below it". Reason: a stepping foot is the whole point of the exercise; the standing rule would fail every frame after the first step. Support is still measured — on whichever foot is loaded.
  - **Add** "the planted foot does not skate": its contact point moves ≤ 0.3 cm/frame horizontally and its yaw stays within 3° while it is loaded. Reason: nothing in the current checks notices a sliding foot, and a 0.55 m step with the wrong interpolation skates badly.
  - **Relax** the squat's fixed balance line: replace "bar over the rear of the foot" with "bar y stays inside the base of support — between the rear heel and the lead toe, at least 4 cm inside each edge — at every frame". Reason: the base of support moves with the stride; a single line computed from a static stance would force the torso to lean as the feet split, which the KB forbids ("posture should remain upright").
  - **Add** "rear knee nearly touches, never touches": at the bottom the rear knee is 3–10 cm above the floor. Reason: KB instruction "Descend until your rear knee nearly touches the ground"; it also stops the support rule being broken silently by a knee through the floor.
  - **Add** "front knee over the ankle": the lead knee's y within ±7 cm of the lead ankle's y at the bottom and the lead shin lean ≤ 25°. Reason: KB cue "Front knee over ankle"; the squat allows 42° of shin lean, which would read as a collapsing knee in a lunge.
  - **Add** "trunk upright": trunk pitch within ±8° of vertical all loop, including the step. Reason: KB cue "Torso tall over hips" and "Your posture should remain upright"; the squat's `plan()` deliberately leans the trunk to chase a balance line and would be copied over by mistake.
  - **Add** "loop seam": frame 1 and frame 72 poses match within 0.5 cm, and the two 1.5 s half-cycles are exact mirrors. Reason: the tile loops 4–6 times in the guide; a seam reads as a stumble.
  - **Split** the `natural_motion_check` knee series per leg (left knee and right knee tracked separately) and keep the 8.0°/frame step and 1.5°/frame² jerk caps on each. Reason: averaging the two legs hides a twitch in one of them, which is exactly the failure mode of a split-stance clip.
  - **Keep unchanged**: bar-on-back gap, grip seat/wrist/fingers/thumb, bone stretch ≤ 2 %, knee/elbow 0–150°, hip flexion ≤ 140°, arm twist caps, tilt 0.4–4° and its step/jerk caps.
- **Safety / form notes for the guide**: "Torso tall over hips"; "Front knee over ankle"; "Push through the front heel". Rear knee comes close to the floor without banging; step length follows the load, not the other way round. The KB longevity note is explicit that lunges are trained for control, not maximal load — the guide should say so, and reps stop 2–3 in reserve.
