# Pull-Up (Bodyweight) — `pull-up-bodyweight`

Status: brief   ·   Author: OpenCode helper   ·   Date: 2026-11-01

_Facts below are taken from the KB entry `pull-up-bodyweight` (bundle 0.6.0): name, equipment, primary/secondary muscles, instructions, cues, common mistakes, safety notes._

- **Pattern / kit**: vertical pull · fixed pull-up bar, bodyweight, no floor support. Copy `scripts/animation/lift_deadlift.py`: it already solves a hand onto a fixed bar axis along world x, wraps the fingers once and measures the grip (`PALM` / `BASE` / `bar_contact` / `wrap_fingers`), and its worked mask is painted on the back. Everything that made the body stand goes: the bar path, the thigh contact rule, the foot support and the floor balance. New set piece: a straight bar on two uprights (reuse the squat's `cylinder_x` bar shaft and `FRAME` uprights at ±0.75 m) plus the existing floor plane. The bar is a hard fixed point in world space for the whole loop.
- **Closest done clip**: `barbell-deadlift` (in progress) for the hand-on-a-bar solve and the back-facing camera; `barbell-squat` for the grip orientation (fingers up, palm forward) and the neon line up the spine. What changes: the bar does not move — the body travels; the arms go from straight to ~115° of elbow flexion; the feet leave the floor; the worked muscles are lats + upper back instead of hamstrings/glutes/erectors.
- **Start / end pose**: he faces −y; the bar runs along x, half grip 0.25 m (KB: "overhand grip slightly wider than shoulder-width"; the shoulders are ~0.40 m apart). Loop start = dead hang (depth 1): elbows 5°, shoulders up near the ears, trunk plumb, hips within 3 cm of the bar's vertical line, knees 5°, ankles 0.12 m apart with the toes pointed ~10° down; the feet clear the floor. Loop end = the same hang after the last lowering. Top (depth 0): chin 3–6 cm above the bar axis, elbows ~110–115° of flexion, elbows down and slightly forward of the shoulders, chest within ~5 cm of the bar, shoulders depressed, trunk still vertical (±5°), legs straight and still — no knee drive, no swing.
- **Movement**: the implement does not move: the bar is static at (0, bar_y, bar_z) with bar_y = shoulder y − 0.03 at the hang. The body travels instead — the shoulder-to-bar distance shortens from `upper_arm + forearm` (straight) to √(l1² + l2² + 2·l1·l2·cos 115°) ≈ 0.31 m, i.e. about 0.26 m of vertical body travel per rep at true bone lengths. Solve it the way `hips_for()` already bisects: pick the hip z that makes `joint_flex(side, "elbow")` equal the target flexion φ(d) (5° → 115°), then solve the arms to the fixed bar with `arm_fk`. Bar height: set bar_z so the lowest body vertex is ≥ 0.10 m above the floor at the dead hang. Tempo over the 3 s loop (0 = chin over bar, 1 = dead hang): 0–0.10 hold at the hang, 0.10–0.46 pull (ease-out), 0.46–0.56 chin-over hold, 0.56–0.92 lower (ease-in-out), 0.92–1.0 hang.
- **Supports** (must touch their surface): the hands are the only support — both palms on the bar (seat ≤ 0.7 cm), fingers hooked over the top (≤ 1 cm), thumb wrapped (≤ 2 cm). The feet, knees, shins and head touch nothing: add a rule that the lowest body vertex stays ≥ 0.10 m above the floor and that no body vertex comes within 2 cm of an upright. The bar is a fixed support point, not a moving implement.
- **Grip / hands**: overhand (pronated), hands 0.50 m apart, wrists neutral (≤ 15°), fingers over the top of the bar with the thumb wrapped round, no false grip. Hand orientation is the squat's: hand points up (+z), palm forward (−y) — `HAND_UP = (0, 0, 1)`, `PALM_N = (0, −1, 0)`; the deadlift's down-pointing constants are wrong for this kit. Wrap the fingers once at the dead hang and keep them keyed to the static bar for the whole loop; the wrist moves with the arm, the hand does not re-solve per frame.
- **Worked muscles (orange)**: primary — lats (the broad sheet from under each armpit to the lower back, both sides) and upper back (the traps and rhomboids between and just below the shoulder blades). Secondary — biceps (not highlighted, even though it will be the most obvious front muscle). Implementation: two regions in the `worked_mask()` style — lat: 0.04 < |x| < 0.22, y > 0 (back half), z between the armpit and the waist, with a falloff toward the spine; upper back: |x| < 0.10, z between the shoulder blades and mid-back, y > 0.02. Reuse the deadlift's erector term and extend it upward rather than inventing a new one; do not paint the arms or the rear delts.
- **Camera**: from behind, three-quarter, his left — yaw 150°, pitch 8°. Reason: the lats and upper back are the primary movers and are only on the back; 150° still shows the elbows driving down and the bar line, while 128° (the deadlift angle) shows too much of the chest at the top. Add the bar's full x extent and both hands to the auto-framing `pts` list at depths 0 and 1, or the hands and bar get cropped.
- **Checks to add or relax** (with the reason; never weaken a rule just to pass):
  - **Replace** the "feet on the floor" support rule with "hands on the bar, minimum body vertex ≥ 0.10 m above the floor, no upright contact". Reason: the exercise is suspended; the standing rule would fail all 72 frames, and the fix is a different support, not a looser tolerance.
  - **Replace** the three bar-path rules (`bar not resting on the floor`, `bar against the thighs at lockout`, `bar passes through the legs`) with "the bar does not move at all; the chin reaches 3–6 cm above the bar at the top and never intersects the bar or the head (bar axis to head skin ≥ 2 cm)". Reason: the implement is bolted to the rig; the body is what travels.
  - **Relax** the elbow range from 0–25° to 0–140°, and require ≥ 100° at the top. Reason: the deadlift's 25° cap enforces straight arms; elbow flexion is the defining action of a pull-up. This is a different movement, not a pass for a bent-arm deadlift.
  - **Add** "no kip, no swing": the ankle centre stays within 0.05 m of the vertical through the shoulder at every frame, the trunk pitch stays within ±8°, and the ankle's y never leads the shoulder's y by more than 0.05 m. Reason: KB mistake "Kipping" and cue "Slow lowering"; the standing clips have pinned feet, so nothing in the current motion check notices a swinging body.
  - **Add** "shoulders depress, do not shrug": the shoulder joint z, measured relative to the head, is at least 1 cm lower at the top than at the dead hang. Reason: KB instruction "Pull your shoulder blades down and back" and mistake "Shrugging".
  - **Swap** the `tilt` series in `natural_motion_check` for an elbow-flexion series (a fixed bar cannot tilt). Keep a step and a jerk cap on it of the same order as the tilt caps (4° of elbow flexion per frame, 1.5°/frame²), and keep the knee and lean series. Reason: the tilt rule exists to stop a rigid look; the equivalent rule for a fixed bar is smooth elbow travel, not a wobbly bar.
  - **Keep unchanged**: bone stretch ≤ 2 %, wrist ≤ 32°, grip seat/finger/thumb, and the arm twist caps (4°/frame step, 1.5°/frame² jerk).
- **Safety / form notes for the guide**: "Chest to the bar"; "Elbows down"; "Slow lowering". No kipping; full range to a dead hang each rep. Use assistance if needed and stop with elbow or shoulder pain. Reps stop 2–3 in reserve; quality over count.
