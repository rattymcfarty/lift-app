_Facts below are taken from the KB entry `overhead-press` (bundle 0.6.0): name, equipment, primary/secondary muscles, instructions, cues, common mistakes, safety notes._

- **Pattern / kit**: vertical push · standing, two dumbbells, floor only (no rack, no barbell). Copy `scripts/animation/lift_squat.py`: it already stands the mannequin on both feet, calibrates straight legs to 99.2 % of reach (`calibrate_top`), pins the ankles and solves a hand onto a world target with `arm_fk` / `solve_side` / `wrap_fingers`. Delete the rack, the platform's rack area and the barbell. Lift the dumbbell mesh (handle + two plates) and its in-hand scale from the bench kit (`lift_toon_scene.py`, private; the `dumbbell-bench-press` clip).
- **Closest done clip**: `dumbbell-bench-press` for the dumbbells in the hands; `barbell-squat` for the standing pose, the floor support, the trunk and the camera. What changes: the implement is two dumbbells instead of one bar, the arms travel from the shoulders to overhead instead of holding a bar on the back, the trunk must stay upright instead of leaning, and the worked muscles are deltoid + triceps instead of quads + glutes. Note the KB entry is **dumbbells/kettlebells**, not a barbell (a barbell vertical push is `military-press-barbell`); build what the entry says.
- **Start / end pose**: loop start = end, standing. Feet shoulder-width (±0.18 m, the squat's `A_T`), toes forward, both soles flat; knees unlocked at the calibrated top angle (no clamp); hips under the ribs, trunk vertical within 2°; head neutral, eyes forward. Start (rack position): each dumbbell handle centre at shoulder height, at the shoulder joint's z, 0.06 m in front of the shoulder joint, ±0.18 m in x; upper arm ~30° below horizontal and ~20° out; elbow flexed 85–95°; hands point up (fingers +z), palms forward. End (same): it is the same pose after the last rep is lowered.
- **Movement**: two dumbbells rise on a near-vertical path from the shoulders to lockout, drifting ~4 cm inward in x at the top and finishing over the shoulder joint (y = shoulder y − 0.02), not in front of the face; the head does not move and the trunk does not lean. Implement path per depth: handle centre y = shoulder y − 0.06 → shoulder y − 0.02; z = shoulder z → shoulder z + (upper_arm + forearm) × cos 8° − 0.02 (~0.10 m above the top of the head); x = ±0.18 → ±0.14. Tempo over the 3 s loop (0 = load at the shoulders, 1 = lockout): 0–0.08 hold at the shoulders, 0.08–0.44 press (ease-out), 0.44–0.56 hold at lockout, 0.56–0.94 lower (ease-in-out), 0.94–1.0 settle at the shoulders.
- **Supports** (must touch their surface): both feet flat on the floor at every frame (float ≤ 0.5 cm, sink ≤ 1.5 cm — the squat's `foot_gap_cm` check, unchanged); each hand on its dumbbell handle (palm seat ≤ 0.7 cm, fingers ≤ 1 cm, thumb ≤ 2 cm — unchanged). Nothing else touches: no rack, no bench, no knee or floor contact. Add: the dumbbell plates must clear the head and neck skin by ≥ 2 cm at every frame (the load passes the face; no existing rule looks at it).
- **Grip / hands**: pronated with the palms forward, so the handle axis stays along world x and the existing `wrap_fingers` / `dist_to_line` / seat maths apply unchanged (a neutral, palms-in grip would need the handle axis parameterised to y — only do that if the owner asks for it). Fingers wrap once at the shoulder position and stay fixed for the whole loop, with the dumbbell rigid in the hand. Wrist within 10° of neutral, never past 15° of extension; the handle sits across the palm, not in the fingers; thumb wrapped, no false grip.
- **Worked muscles (orange)**: primary — anterior deltoid (front of each shoulder cap) and triceps (back of each upper arm, shoulder to elbow). Secondary — chest, abs, upper back (not highlighted). Implementation: two regions in the `worked_mask()` style on rest-pose geometry — deltoid: a 0.07 m ball around the shoulder joint head, anterior half only (surface normal · (0, −1, 0) > 0); triceps: t ∈ [0.15, 0.85] along the humerus axis from the shoulder head, off-axis point behind the bone (off.y > 0) within the upper-arm radius. Paint the upper arm only; bleeding the triceps region onto the forearm reads as a forearm exercise.
- **Camera**: three-quarter front from his left, slightly above the shoulder line — yaw 28°, pitch 10°. Reason: the front delt faces the camera at this angle and the near arm's lateral triceps head shows on the outline; from behind (yaw > 100°) the delts are invisible, and a pure side view hides the far arm. Add the dumbbells at depth 0 and 1 to the auto-framing `pts` list, or the top of the press is cropped in the square tile.
- **Checks to add or relax** (with the reason; never weaken a rule just to pass):
  - **Drop** the squat's `bar_back_gap_cm` and `bar_over_foot_cm` checks: there is no bar on the back and the load is not one rigid line over the foot. Replace them with the two rules below, not with nothing.
  - **Add** "trunk never leans back": trunk pitch ≥ −5° (forward) in the y–z plane at every frame, measured on the spine bone against +z. Reason: KB cue "Ribs down, glutes squeezed — no lean-back" and mistake "Arching the lower back to heave the weight up"; the squat's balance solve produces forward lean and would happily pass a press that arches.
  - **Add** "load over the base": each handle centre stays between the heel and the toe in y at every frame, and within 0.05 m of the shoulder joint in y at lockout. Reason: KB mistake "Pressing the weights out in front instead of overhead"; this is the press's version of the old balance rule.
  - **Add** "no shrug": shoulder joint z at lockout ≤ its value in the rack position + 2 cm. Reason: KB mistake "Shrugging the shoulders toward the ears at the top".
  - **Relax** the attitude check for two independent hand-held loads: per-dumbbell tilt about x up to 6° (was 4° for one bar), divergence between the two ≤ 4°, and keep the 0.35°/frame step and 0.2°/frame² jerk caps exactly as they are. Reason: two free weights in two hands are not one welded bar; holding both to a single-bar tolerance makes them look mechanically linked. This is a physical property, not a way past a failure.
  - **Keep unchanged**: bone stretch ≤ 2 %, elbow and knee 0–150°, foot support, grip seat/wrist/fingers/thumb, and the knee and lean step/jerk limits in `natural_motion_check`.
- **Safety / form notes for the guide**: "Ribs down, glutes squeezed — no lean-back"; "Press up and slightly in, biceps finishing by your ears"; "Lower with control to the starting position every rep". Load so 3 sets of 10 finish at RPE 7 with no back arch. With shoulder impingement keep every rep pain-free and stop short of a pinching lockout; never press behind the neck. Reps stop 2–3 in reserve.
