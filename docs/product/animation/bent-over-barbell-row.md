# Bent Over Barbell Row — `bent-over-barbell-row`

Status: brief   ·   Author: OpenCode helper   ·   Date: 2026-11-01

_Facts below are taken from the KB entry `bent-over-barbell-row` (bundle 0.6.0): name, equipment, primary/secondary muscles, instructions, cues, safety notes. The KB display name is "Bent Over Barbell Row" (no hyphen); ANIMATION_PLAN shortens it._

- **Pattern / kit**: horizontal pull · floor + barbell, hinged torso (reference script: `scripts/animation/lift_deadlift.py`). Keep the barbell, the lifting platform, the camera rig, the hand-on-bar solve (`PALM` / `BASE` / `solve_side` / `wrap_fingers`) and the grip checks. Change the movement solve: the torso is nailed at the hinge angle and the hips do not travel; the bar rides an arc up and in toward the abdomen while the elbows bend.
- **Closest done clip**: `barbell-deadlift` — same bar, same hinge, same hands, same platform. What changes: the trunk holds ~70° instead of sweeping from upright to the floor, the hips stay put, the implement path goes from under the shoulders to the lower ribs, the arms flex 0 → ~100°, and the worked muscles are the upper back instead of hamstrings/glutes/erectors.
- **Start / end pose**: loop start = end = bottom, arms extended. Feet hip-width (±0.13 m, the deadlift's `STANCE`), toes forward, both soles flat; knees bent 20–25°; hips pushed back to the deadlift's `HY_BOT` so the trunk holds 65–75° from vertical (KB: "until it is almost parallel to the floor"), back flat, chest up, neck extended ~20° back from the trunk line so the eyes look forward-down rather than at the floor. Bar hangs from straight arms under the shoulder line, about knee height. Top: bar 0–2 cm from the abdominal skin, 8–12 cm below the shoulder line measured along the trunk; elbows flexed ~95–105°, elbows behind and close to the ribs; torso and hips unchanged.
- **Movement**: the bar rises ~0.30 m and comes in ~0.15 m toward the torso, following an arc (up and in), not a vertical line; the shoulders, elbows and wrists stay in one plane per side, and the wrists do not lead the elbows. Tempo over the 3 s loop (0 = bottom, 1 = squeezed at the abdomen): 0–0.10 hold at the bottom, 0.10–0.42 pull (ease-out), 0.42–0.54 squeeze at the top, 0.54–0.92 lower (ease-in-out), 0.92–1.0 bottom hold. Implementation note: the deadlift bisects the hip height to put straight arms on a given bar point; here the hips are fixed, so invert it — pose the body once, then for each depth solve the arm forward at the target elbow flexion φ(d) (0° → 100°) and put the bar where the palm lands. That keeps the hands on the bar without letting the torso move.
- **Supports** (must touch their surface): both feet flat on the floor all loop (float ≤ 0.5 cm, sink ≤ 1.5 cm — unchanged); both hands on the bar (palm seat ≤ 0.7 cm, fingers ≤ 1 cm, thumb ≤ 2 cm — unchanged). Nothing else: the bar must not touch the shins, knees, thighs or abdomen except at the top contact, and the head touches nothing.
- **Grip / hands**: pronated (palms down, KB instruction), half grip 0.22 m so the hands clear the knees, bar across the palm, thumbs wrapped (no false grip), wrists within 15° of neutral — a cocked wrist is the usual row cheat and there is no rule catching it today. Fingers wrap once at the bottom and stay put; the bar is rigid in the hand. `HAND_UP` stays the deadlift's (0, 0, −1) with `PALM_N = (0, 1, 0)`; keep the hand-roll sign fixed at the first solve (a flip is a 180° wrist step).
- **Worked muscles (orange)**: primary — upper back (the traps and rhomboids between and just under the shoulder blades, both sides). Secondary — lats, biceps, front delts (not highlighted: the KB files lats as secondary for this entry, so do not paint the lat sheet, and do not paint the arms). Implementation: one region in the `worked_mask()` style — |x| < 0.16, y > 0 (back half), z from the shoulder line down 0.28 m, strongest along the medial border of each scapula and fading out toward the spine and under the armpit.
- **Camera**: three-quarter from behind his left, looking down onto the bent back — yaw 118°, pitch 22°. Reason: when he hinges, the upper back faces up and back; the deadlift's 128°/14° puts the head and bar in front of the worked area and flattens the shoulder blades. The auto-framing list must include the bar at both depths, the head and both feet, or the bottom of the row is cropped.
- **Checks to add or relax** (with the reason; never weaken a rule just to pass):
  - **Replace** the deadlift's "bar over mid-foot" balance rule with "the combined body + bar centre of mass stays over the mid-foot ±4 cm, and the bar is allowed to hang up to 0.25 m in front of that line". Reason: a row's bar hangs from the shoulders, well forward of the balance line; applying the deadlift rule directly would force the torso upright and destroy the exercise. Balance is still checked — on the COM, not on the bar.
  - **Relax** the elbow range from 0–25° to 0–135° and require ≥ 85° at the top. Reason: the row is an elbow-flexion lift; the deadlift's cap exists to keep the arms straight under a bar that is being lifted by the legs.
  - **Replace** "bar against the thighs at lockout" / "bar passes through the legs" with: the bar never intersects the shins, knees, thighs or torso (any negative gap fails); at the bottom the bar is ≥ 2 cm in front of the shins; at the top the bar is 0–2 cm from the abdomen. Reason: the contact point moves from the thighs (deadlift) to the abdomen (row), and the "against the thighs" rule would fail a correct row at every frame.
  - **Add** "torso stationary": trunk pitch varies ≤ 2° across the loop and the hip centre moves ≤ 1 cm. Reason: KB instruction "while keeping the torso stationary"; the deadlift's solve sweeps the trunk and its motion check would not object to a heaving row.
  - **Add** "elbows to the ribs": at the top, |x_elbow − x_shoulder| ≤ 0.10 m and the elbow is behind the shoulder (y_elbow > y_shoulder). Reason: KB instruction "keep the elbows close to the body" and cue "Pull the elbows to the ribs"; nothing today constrains elbow flare.
  - **Add** "no shrug, no lower-back heave": the shoulder joint z stays within 1.5 cm of its bottom-frame value relative to the torso, and the hip z within 1 cm. Reason: KB cue "Chest up, shoulders down" plus "Squeeze at the top"; without it the row turns into a light deadlift.
  - **Add** a `bary` series next to the existing `barz` series (same 0.02 m/frame step and 0.004 m/frame² jerk caps). Reason: the row's in-and-up arc is mostly in y; the deadlift only smooths the vertical component.
  - **Keep unchanged**: tilt 0.4–4°, tilt step/jerk caps, bone stretch ≤ 2 %, knee 0–150°, foot support, grip seat/wrist/fingers/thumb, and the arm twist caps.
- **Safety / form notes for the guide**: "Chest up, shoulders down"; "Pull the elbows to the ribs"; "Squeeze at the top". Keep the load light enough that the torso does not move — the KB start position asks for a straight back with the head up, so no jerking and no rounding. If the lower back starts doing the work, the lean is too deep or the load too heavy. Reps stop 2–3 in reserve.
